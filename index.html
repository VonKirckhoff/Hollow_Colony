    CAMPAIGN · LUNAR CORPS

    Operation Hollow Light

    The Osprey went down at Hollow Colony and its crew is listed as killed in action. They are not dead. Seven soldiers, no base, no dropship, hunted by the Brood and by the Corps. Harvest Brood DNA, splice it into the crew between jobs, and keep the taint from eating them alive.

    Play the same story from the other side. You are the Brood: you choose what hatches, where it gathers and where it strikes, and the swarm does the rest. The Lunar Corps is played by the computer.

        Controls

        Select

        Left drag
        Box-select your units. Click one to select it, double-click for all of that type on screen. Shift adds.
        Ctrl + 1–5
        Save a control group; 1–5 recalls it, twice jumps to it.
        Panel hover
        Point at a soldier in the selection panel to light him up on the map and minimap; a group name lights up the group.

        Orders

        Right click
        Move, attack an enemy, pick up a crate, repair (Engineer) or set a rally point.
        A + click
        Attack: move and fight on the way. Stays on for later moves.
        H
        Defensive: hold and fire at what comes in range; holds again after every move. H again: off.
        Q
        Hold fire: they do not shoot on their own, not even back, only at what you right-click. Q again, A or S: weapons free.
        S
        Cancel order and drop the stance.
        L + click
        Throw a flare (Trooper, Gunner or Pyro).

        Squad

        Z / X / V / I / W
        Formation: circle, wedge, square, line or zigzag. Support in the centre (behind a line). The squad marches in the shape; a new one is taken up on the way. Same key again releases it. A control group remembers its formation.
        Y / F / E
        Your own formation / save the squad as it stands as your own formation / spacing: normal, wide or tight.
        Sweep
        Attack (A) with a squad in a line: they advance together and fire on the move.
        Right-drag
        A squad in formation goes where you pressed and faces the way you dragged.
        F9 or ² / `
        Dev panel: see and tune the game's numbers while you play, pause, save your changes.
        U / D
        Engineer: pack up a Sentry Gun and deploy it elsewhere.
        R / T / B
        Engineer: build a Relay Tower, Sentry Gun or Barricade.

        View and sound

        Arrows, edge
        Scroll. Middle-drag also works; click or drag the minimap to jump.
        Space
        Jump to the latest alert.
        C / O
        Camera follow on/off, voices on/off. Light and sound: Menu → Settings.
        P / Esc
        Pause. Esc also cancels a pending click.

        Field notes

        Firing on the moveSoldiers keep walking but shoot back at anything in range, a bit slower than standing still. Engineers do the same while they build or repair.
        CoverRock or a building between a soldier and a Spitter cuts the damage he takes by 40%.
        SuppressionAnything a Gunner hits slows down for a moment.
        Acid bloodBrood bleeds acid. Killing one up close burns your soldiers, and the pool keeps burning for 3 seconds.
        CocoonsYour dead are turned into cocoons that hatch in seconds. Burn them; a Pyro does triple damage.
        Medic triageThe downed first, then the worst wounded. His sidearm only comes out when nobody nearby needs him.
        Down, not outA soldier at 0 health goes down for 15 seconds. The Brood leaves him be: it wants the body. A Medic who reaches him patches him up in 4 seconds. Nobody comes, and he bleeds out.
        The fallenThe Brood drags a body home to the mycel around a hive. There an egg opens, a Grafter latches on, and the body becomes a cocoon. Stand next to it, kill the carrier, shoot the eggs, or let a Pyro burn it.
        HusksAn infected soldier. It hatches as a Twitcher, molts into a Shambler, then into a Husk; while it molts it takes extra damage. A Husk slips away to a quiet spot and takes root. Kill it before a new hive grows there.
        Darkness and flaresIn dark missions everyone sees half as far. Flares light the ground for 45 seconds: three per mission, six in the dark.
        Motion trackerShows Brood moving near your selected squad, even in the dark, and beeps faster as they close in.
        Brood tacticsBig waves may send bait first, or split up and come at you from the side.
        Hives fight backCome too close and a hive notices you: its guards attack and it births a brood. Every hit makes it scream for more; badly hurt, it calls the other hives. Left alone, it heals.
        The mycelA purple fungus spreads around every hive. The Brood heals while standing on it. It withers when the hive dies.
        Landing padsOrder replacements from the pad. Never more than the squad you started with.
        IntelEvery soldier, structure and enemy with its numbers: see Intel in the main menu.

        Factions

        Lunar CorpsYou
        • Salvage from crates and from Relay Towers on relay pads. Each tower adds supply.
        • Train soldiers at the Command Post, or order them in at a landing pad.
        • Troopers, Gunners and Pyros fight; Medics heal; Engineers build, repair and arm charges.
        The BroodEnemy
        • Grows from hives and comes in waves that get bigger.
        • Crawlers and Scuttlers rush, Spitters lob acid from range, Brutes break walls.
        • Everything living it kills becomes a cocoon: new Brood.
        • In its own campaign you are the Brood: hatch, hunt and bring the fallen home.
        Rogue StrainThird campaign
        • The seven survivors of the Osprey, written off as killed in action. They found Dr. Maas's samples and stopped answering the radio.
        • No base, no dropship, no replacements: the same seven people from job to job, led by their Captain.
        • They splice Brood DNA into their own blood. Strains make them faster, tougher or deadlier; taint makes them stranger, up to a feral fit.
        • Black and dark brown uniforms with a red armband.
        • Hunted by both sides: the Brood, and the loyal Corps in the normal Corps kit, who shoot on sight.
        The loyal CorpsIn Rogue Strain the Lunar Corps is the enemy. Its soldiers fight the Brood and the crew alike: they hold a post, patrol or hunt you, and come running when one of them is hit. Their Relay Towers, Sentry Guns and Barricades are theirs too.
        Playable after the Corps campaignRogue Strain opens once every Lunar Corps campaign mission is won on Normal or Hard. Its campaign screen and Intel tab can be read before that.

        Veterans

        Earning XPKills (a Spitter 2, a Brute 4, a hive 5). Medics earn by healing, Engineers by building and repairing.
        Lieutenant abilitiesTrooper grenade · Gunner steady aim on the move · Pyro long flame · Medic heals everyone around him · Engineer works twice as fast.
        Your own soldierEnlist him on the campaign screen. He goes on every mission and only he can become Captain: soldiers near him fire faster and take less damage.
        The rosterSurvivors with XP carry over to the next campaign mission and replace starting soldiers of their type.
        CenotaphThe fallen are cut into stone. In zombie mode you can raise them once.

        Campaigns

        TrainingBoth campaigns start with a training (00) that walks you through every button. Press N to skip a step.
        Playing the BroodPick The Brood on the campaign screen. Q W E R hatch, right-click sets the gathering point (or moves the swarm units you selected), A + click attacks, H brings the fallen home, B + click burrows up a small brood. Ctrl + a number keeps a group: flank or lure with two groups of your own.
        Rogue StrainOpens once every Lunar Corps campaign mission is won on Normal or Hard. You play the seven survivors of the Osprey. Brood you kill without fire can leave DNA samples: walk over them. Between jobs, splice them into the crew on the campaign screen. Taint makes them stronger and stranger: from 85% seizures, at 100% a feral fit. A crew Medic with a stabilizer dose fixes that (J). The crew wears black and dark brown with a red armband; soldiers in the normal Corps kit are the loyal Corps, and they shoot you on sight.
        Trophy wallWin every campaign mission with your own soldier alive, for each role and each difficulty. Flawless: win a mission without losing a soldier, one per difficulty. Two grand trophies: all of the Lunar Corps, and every Brood mission on every difficulty.
