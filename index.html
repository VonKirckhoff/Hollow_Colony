        Controls

        Select

        Left drag
        Box-select your units. Click one to select it, double-click for all of that type on screen. Shift adds.
        Ctrl + 1–5
        Save a control group; 1–5 recalls it, twice jumps to it.
        Panel hover
        Point at a soldier in the selection panel to light him up on the map and minimap; a group name lights up the group.

        Orders

        Right click
        Move, attack an enemy, pick up a crate, repair (Engineer) or set a rally point.
        A + click
        Attack: move and fight on the way. Stays on for later moves.
        H
        Defensive: hold and fire at what comes in range; holds again after every move. H again: off.
        Q
        Hold fire: they do not shoot on their own, not even back, only at what you right-click. Q again, A or S: weapons free.
        S
        Cancel order and drop the stance.
        L + click
        Throw a flare (Trooper, Gunner or Pyro).

        Squad

        Z / X / V / I / W
        Formation: circle, wedge, square, line or zigzag. Support in the centre (behind a line). Same key again releases it. A control group remembers its formation.
        Y / F / E
        Your own formation / save the squad as it stands as your own formation / spacing: normal, wide or tight.
        Sweep
        Attack (A) with a squad in a line: they advance together and fire on the move.
        Right-drag
        A squad in formation goes where you pressed and faces the way you dragged.
        F9 or ² / `
        Dev panel: see and tune the game's numbers while you play, pause, save your changes.
        U / D
        Engineer: pack up a Sentry Gun and deploy it elsewhere.
        R / T / B
        Engineer: build a Relay Tower, Sentry Gun or Barricade.

        View and sound

        Arrows, edge
        Scroll. Middle-drag also works; click or drag the minimap to jump.
        Space
        Jump to the latest alert.
        C / K / O
        Camera follow, motion tracker, voices on/off.
        P / Esc
        Pause. Esc also cancels a pending click.

        Field notes

        Firing on the moveSoldiers keep walking but shoot back at anything in range, a bit slower than standing still. Engineers do the same while they build or repair.
        CoverRock or a building between a soldier and a Spitter or Hunter cuts the damage he takes by 40%.
        SuppressionAnything a Gunner hits slows down for a moment.
        Acid bloodSwarm bleeds acid. Killing one up close burns your soldiers, and the pool keeps burning for 3 seconds.
        CocoonsYour dead are turned into cocoons that hatch in seconds. Burn them; a Pyro does triple damage.
        Medic triageThe downed first, then the worst wounded. His sidearm only comes out when nobody nearby needs him.
        Down, not outA soldier at 0 health goes down for 15 seconds. The Swarm leaves him be: it wants the body. A Medic who reaches him patches him up in 4 seconds. Nobody comes, and he bleeds out.
        The fallenThe Swarm drags a body home to the creep around a hive. There an egg opens, a Grafter latches on, and the body becomes a cocoon. Stand next to it, kill the carrier, shoot the eggs, or let a Pyro burn it.
        HusksAn infected soldier. It hatches as a Twitcher, molts into a Shambler, then into a Husk; while it molts it takes extra damage. A Husk slips away to a quiet spot and takes root. Kill it before a new hive grows there.
        Darkness and flaresIn dark missions everyone sees half as far. Flares light the ground for 45 seconds: three per mission, six in the dark.
        Motion trackerShows Swarm moving near your selected squad, even in the dark, and beeps faster as they close in.
        Swarm tacticsBig waves may send bait first, or split up and come at you from the side.
        Hives fight backCome too close and a hive notices you: its guards attack and it births a brood. Every hit makes it scream for more; badly hurt, it calls the other hives. Left alone, it heals.
        The creepA purple fungus spreads around every hive. The Swarm heals while standing on it. It withers when the hive dies.
        Landing padsOrder replacements from the pad. Never more than the squad you started with.
        IntelEvery soldier, structure and enemy with its numbers: see Intel in the main menu.

        Factions

        Lunar CorpsYou
        • Salvage from crates and from Relay Towers on relay pads. Each tower adds supply.
        • Train soldiers at the Command Post, or order them in at a landing pad.
        • Troopers, Gunners and Pyros fight; Medics heal; Engineers build, repair and arm charges.
        The SwarmEnemy
        • Grows from hives and comes in waves that get bigger.
        • Crawlers and Drones rush, Spitters lob acid from range, Brutes break walls.
        • Everything living it kills becomes a cocoon: new Swarm.
        The GreyUnknown
        • Cloaked hunters, seen only when they fire, are hit or come close.
        • They hunt your strongest soldier and fall back when wounded.
        • Not playable yet.

        Veterans

        Earning XPKills (a Spitter 2, a Brute 4, a hive 5). Medics earn by healing, Engineers by building and repairing.
        Lieutenant abilitiesTrooper grenade · Gunner steady aim on the move · Pyro long flame · Medic heals everyone around him · Engineer works twice as fast.
        Your own soldierEnlist him on the campaign screen. He goes on every mission and only he can become Captain: soldiers near him fire faster and take less damage.
        The rosterSurvivors with XP carry over to the next campaign mission and replace starting soldiers of their type.
        CenotaphThe fallen are cut into stone. In zombie mode you can raise them once.
        TrainingBoth campaigns start with a training (00) that walks you through every button. Press N to skip a step.
        Playing the SwarmPick The Swarm on the campaign screen. Q W E R hatch, right-click sets the gathering point (or moves the swarm units you selected), A + click attacks, H brings the fallen home, T picks a tactic, B + click burrows up a small brood.
        Trophy wallWin every campaign mission with your own soldier alive, for each role and each difficulty, and Real death too. Two grand trophies: all of the Lunar Corps, and every Swarm mission on every difficulty.
        Start fresh

        Erase everything this game keeps in your browser: campaign progress, veterans, your own soldier, the Cenotaph, difficulty, the defeat choice, zombie mode, and all music, sound and voice settings. The game starts as if you opened it for the first time.

        Veterans

        Soldiers who survive a campaign mission with experience stay on the roster. At the start of the next mission they replace soldiers of the same type, and the rest arrive first when you train or order that type. A veteran who falls goes on the Cenotaph.

        LUNAR CORPS · INTEL

        Intel

        What every soldier, structure and enemy can do. Range and sight are in tiles, speed in tiles per second; damage per second is before rank bonuses.

        LUNAR CORPS · HALL OF HONOUR

        Trophy wall

        One trophy for every role and every difficulty. To earn it, one soldier of your own must start at mission 1 and win every campaign mission in order, in that role, walking out of each one alive. Progress belongs to that soldier: if they fall, the run is over. A soldier enlisted later never qualifies. A win on Hard also counts for Normal and Easy. Real death counts only missions won with real death on. Below them hang two grand trophies: all of the Lunar Corps, and every Swarm mission on every difficulty.

        LUNAR CORPS · TYCHO MEMORIAL

        Cenotaph

        An empty tomb for the soldiers who never came home. Every name is cut into the stone, with the mission where it fell.
