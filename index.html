      Controls

      Left drag
      Select your units with a box. Click a unit to select it, double-click to select all of that type on screen. Shift adds to the selection.
      Right click
      Move, attack an enemy, pick up a salvage crate, repair a structure (engineers) or set a rally point (Command Post).
      A + click
      Attack-move: walk there and fight anything on the way.
      S / H
      Stop / hold position.
      Ctrl + 1–5
      Save a control group. Press 1–5 to recall it.
      Arrows, screen edge
      Scroll the map. Middle-drag also works. Click or drag on the minimap to jump.
      Space
      Jump to the latest alert.
      Z / X / V
      Formation for the selected squad: circle, wedge or square. Medics and Engineers go in the centre, Pyros and Gunners take the tip of the wedge. The squad keeps the shape when it moves. Press the same key again to release it.
      U / D
      Engineer: pack up a Sentry Gun (or right-click an undamaged one) and deploy it again somewhere else. A carrying Engineer walks at 60% speed.
      C
      Camera follow on/off. When on, the camera moves along with your squad after each order. Scrolling yourself pauses it until the next order.
      P / Esc
      Pause menu. Esc also cancels building placement.

      Factions

      Lunar CorpsEarn salvage from Relay Towers built on relay pads and from crates on the field. Each tower also adds supply. Engineers build, Medics heal.
      The SwarmGrows from hives. Anything living it kills becomes a cocoon that hatches into new Swarm. Destroy cocoons fast; Pyros do triple damage to them.
      VeteransEvery soldier has a name and earns XP by killing (Medics by healing, Engineers by building). Ranks Corporal, Sergeant and Veteran add HP, damage and rate of fire. Survivors carry over to the next campaign mission; the fallen are gone for good.
      The GreyHunters who earn renown through honourable kills. Not yet deployable in this version.
